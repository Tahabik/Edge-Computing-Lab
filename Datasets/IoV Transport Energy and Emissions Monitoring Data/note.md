### About Dataset

These two datasets provide complementary insights into urban transportation energy use and environmental impact over time within the context of the Internet of Vehicles (IoV) ecosystem.

Dataset-1, EV Charging Demand, captures electric vehicle charging power demand at a hospital charging site with 15-minute granularity spanning mid-2018 to mid-2019, including calendar and weather features.

Dataset-2, Urban Transport Emissions Monitoring, offers minute-level data on transportation-related carbon emissions with traffic parameters such as vehicle count, road length, average speed, and engine load factor.

Preprocessing was performed using Min-Max Normalization, and Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) was applied for feature extraction to capture periodic patterns and enhance signal-based analysis.

IoV enables vehicles, including electric vehicles (EVs), to connect with the power grid and traffic management systems in real time. EVs leverage IoV technologies for smart charging, sharing detailed energy usage data, and even feeding energy back to the grid through Vehicle-to-Grid (V2G) systems.

Dataset-2 reflects the broader traffic conditions and vehicular behavior within this connected ecosystem, allowing for analysis of how road usage and traffic flows influence emissions.

Together, these datasets support comprehensive studies of transportation energy consumption, emissions, and the role of IoV in sustainable urban mobility.
